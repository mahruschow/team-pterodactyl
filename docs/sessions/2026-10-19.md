## Findings worth sharing

- Routing supply current with its return directly beneath it matters more than distance for the BNO055. An unpaired 0.3 A conductor at 40 mm is about 1.5 µT (about 5 magnetometer resolution steps); paired over a plane it is under 0.01 µT.
- Estimated I2C rise time with only the two host-side 10 kΩ pull-ups is about 340 ns at about 80 pF. That is fine for 100 kHz but over the 300 ns fast-mode limit, so do not use 400 kHz without measuring.
- A sealed enclosure would give wrong MS8607 pressure readings. Outside-air temperature/humidity needs exposure; remote probes were excluded in D01.
- The GPS needs a sky-facing antenna and an RF-transparent lid. Flight orientation of the board is an open question (R6).
