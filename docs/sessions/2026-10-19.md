## Findings worth sharing

- Routing supply current with its return directly beneath it matters more than distance for the BNO055. An unpaired 0.3 A conductor at 40 mm is about 1.5 µT (about 5 magnetometer resolution steps); paired over a plane it is under 0.01 µT.
- Estimated I2C rise time with only the two host-side 10 kΩ pull-ups is about 340 ns at about 80 pF. That is fine for 100 kHz but over the 300 ns fast-mode limit, so do not use 400 kHz without measuring.
- A sealed enclosure would give wrong MS8607 pressure readings. Outside-air temperature/humidity needs exposure; remote probes were excluded in D01.
- The GPS needs a sky-facing antenna and an RF-transparent lid. Flight orientation of the board is an open question (R6).

## Step 5 — power architecture and draft v4 schematic

Saadat asked to move on to Step 5.

1. Traced the legacy power design from the exported netlist (read-only). Findings F1–F6 in POWER_ARCHITECTURE.md:
   - Two regulator outputs are switched straight onto VCC with no OR-ing.
   - Nothing isolates Teensy VIN from USB power.
   - The status LEDs have no series resistors.
   - The voltage monitor reads regulated 5 V, not the battery.
   - There is no fuse or reverse protection.
   - The A17 net's purpose is unknown.
2. Confirmed Teensy 4.1 limits on the PJRC page: pins not 5 V tolerant, 4 mA per-pin recommendation, 250 mA external 3.3 V, cut the VUSB/VIN pads for external power. Confirmed the Pololu S9V11F5 page: 2–16 V in, 5 V out, 1.5 A, no reverse protection, EN pull-up. The S9V11F5 physical pinout was not confirmed.
3. Wrote the load budget (about 175 mA typical / 260 mA peak at 5 V; about 1 W from the battery) and the proposed architecture. Asked three questions. Saadat chose the battery-agnostic regulator, dropping the expansion headers, and a new clean schematic.
4. Built `Native_KiCad_v4/`:
   - `tools/generate_v4_schematic.py` writes the schematic, a v4 symbol library (logical regulator, Teensy with corrected power pin types) and project library tables. The tables use `${KICAD10_*}` paths, because this machine has no global KiCad library table.
   - `tools/sexpr.py` is a small S-expression helper.
   - Two design changes were made while building: the main switch became an off-board panel switch on a connector (the library slide switches are rated about 100 mA), and the LED colours changed to low-Vf types with 680 Ω resistors (white/blue would barely light from 3.3 V).
